Evolution

From rapid PoCs to systemic cloud redesigns.

Overview

Navigating the complexities of automotive software to deliver real-world impact. This collection highlights my work at ETAS, ranging from rapid PoCs and Restbus simulations to scalable cloud redesigns and GTM strategies.

Role

UX & Interaction Designer

Duration

12 months

Deliverables

B2B Cloud Platform & Desktop Softwares

Trade Show PoC: Visioning the Future of INCA

Partnered directly with the Business Manager to conceptualize and design a new real-time collaboration feature for the existing INCA software. The goal was to deliver a high-fidelity prototype and a marketing video to showcase our innovative capabilities and validate user interest live at the Automotive Testing Expo.

Streamlining Fleet Operations

Redesigned the legacy fleet management platform to streamline remote test data transmission and improve the monitoring of vehicles and devices.

Driving End-to-End SaaS Optimization

Driving the UX lifecycle from problem identification to technical release.

  • Created interactive Figma prototypes and official explanatory videos.
  • Restructured information architecture and conducted usability tests.
  • Guided technical implementation and resolved 50+ UX bugs.
*ETAS Cloud Hub Documentation

Restbus Simulation:
Redefining Technical Interaction

Bridging technical complexity through research-driven design. Collaborated with PMs to define scope and map out intricate interaction logic. Delivered a high-fidelity clickable prototype for target user testing to validate the core simulation experience.

Systematizing Brand Communication

Standardizing the ETAS visual identity through precision iconography, explanatory diagrams, and high-impact marketing banners. Each asset was crafted to simplify complex technical logic while ensuring 100% styleguide consistency across every touchpoint.
